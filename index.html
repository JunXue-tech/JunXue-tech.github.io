    I am currently a Ph.D. Candidate at the National Cybersecurity School, Wuhan University, focusing on AI-driven speech processing, deepfake detection, and secure human-computer interaction.

    After completing my master's studies, I worked as an AI Speech Algorithm Engineer at iFLYTEK Research, where I engaged in research and development of cutting-edge speech technologies.

    My research aims to move towards a new generation of human-computer interaction centered on humans. This new generation of interaction should not only involve the exchange of information, but also achieve breakthroughs in emotion, cognition, and interaction forms.

    During my master's studies, I conducted research on fake speech detection at the IIP-HCI Lab of Anhui University, under the supervision of Prof. Zhao Lv and Dr. Cunhang Fan.
